How it works
Each shot makes a keyframe (a still) and a clip from it - or continues the shot before it (extend keeps the look and the size best; last frame is a plain chain). Make one shot at a time and look at it, then the next. Cut joins the clips, trimmed to each shot's seconds, with their sound. A starter opens as your own private copy; its shots that already have a finished clip use it until you press Make my own. Text in {braces} is filled from Cast and look. Keep extend chains to about 3 hops, then start a shot from a fresh keyframe.
